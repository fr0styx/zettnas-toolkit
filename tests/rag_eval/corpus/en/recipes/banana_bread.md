# Grandma's banana bread

Makes one loaf. Mash three very ripe bananas with a fork. Stir in 75 g melted butter,
150 g brown sugar, one egg and a teaspoon of vanilla. Fold in 190 g plain flour,
one teaspoon of baking soda and a pinch of salt. Add a handful of chopped walnuts if
you like. Pour into a greased loaf tin and bake at 175 °C for 55–60 minutes, until a
skewer comes out clean. Let it cool for ten minutes before turning it out.
Keeps for four days wrapped in foil, or freeze slices for up to three months.
