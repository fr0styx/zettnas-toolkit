Japan, 3-17 April

Fly London Heathrow to Tokyo Haneda on JAL 44, landing 3 April at 18:05.

Tokyo (4 nights)

Hotel Gracery Shinjuku. Day trip to Nikko on day 3. Buy the 7-day JR Pass before leaving; activate it on 7 April.

Kyoto (5 nights)

Ryokan near Gion. Fushimi Inari early in the morning to avoid crowds; Arashiyama bamboo grove; tea ceremony booked for 10 April at 14:00.

Osaka (3 nights)

Street food in Dotonbori, day trip to Nara to see the deer.

Return flight from Kansai on 17 April.
