Boiler service notes - Vaillant ecoTEC plus 832

Annual service due every October. Last service 12 Oct, engineer Dave Morris, Gas Safe 581233.
Pressure should sit between 1.0 and 1.5 bar when cold. If it drops below 0.8 the boiler
shows fault F22. To repressurise: open the filling loop under the boiler slowly until the
gauge reads 1.2 bar, then close both valves. Bleed radiators upstairs first if they are cold
at the top. Fault F28 means ignition failed - check the gas meter is on, then press reset.
Warranty runs until March 2029 as long as the yearly service is done.
